Citation Checker

Paste a reference list. Every PubMed ID and DOI is checked against PubMed and Crossref, so made-up or mislabeled citations stand out.

Numbered, one per line, or separated by blank lines. Up to 150.

Faster lookups (optional email)

Crossref answers faster when you include an email. It goes only to Crossref and isn’t stored.

Not a substitute for reading the cited papers. Always open the link before relying on a result.

Sources: PubMed, Europe PMC and Crossref. Your references go only to these services and aren’t stored.

How it decides

Verified: the ID exists and most of its title words appear in your reference. Wrong ID: the ID exists but belongs to a different paper. Not found: the ID doesn’t exist, or a title search found nothing close. Matching is automatic and can be wrong.
