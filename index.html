Citation Checker

Paste a reference list. Each reference is checked against PubMed and Crossref to confirm the paper exists and that its PubMed ID (PMID) or DOI points to the paper the reference describes. Useful for catching made-up or mislabeled citations, including those produced by AI tools.

Works with numbered lists, one reference per line, or references separated by blank lines. Up to 150 references per run.

Optional: your email (speeds up Crossref lookups)

Crossref routes requests that include a contact email to faster servers. It is sent only to Crossref with your queries and is not stored anywhere.

How it decides. "Verified" means the ID exists and most of the found paper's title words appear in your reference. "Wrong ID" means the ID exists but belongs to a different paper. "Not found" means the ID does not exist, or a title search found nothing close. Matching is automatic and can be wrong, so always open the link before relying on a result.

Privacy. Your references are sent only to the public PubMed (NCBI), Europe PMC, and Crossref services to look them up. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else.
